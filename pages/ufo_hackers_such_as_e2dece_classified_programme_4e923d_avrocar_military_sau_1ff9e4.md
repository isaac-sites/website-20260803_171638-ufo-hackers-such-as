## Introduction

The Avro Canada VZ-9AV Avrocar is one of the few aircraft in military history that genuinely looked like the classic "flying saucer" described in UFO reports. Unlike many supposed secret disc-shaped craft, the Avrocar was not a rumour or conspiracy theory but a documented Cold War research programme backed by the United States Army, the United States Air Force and Avro Canada. Its circular shape was not chosen to imitate science fiction. It was an engineering solution intended to create a vertical take-off and landing (VTOL) aircraft capable of hovering, accelerating rapidly and eventually flying at high speed. In practice, however, the aircraft never achieved those ambitions. Its documented shortcomings make it an important case study in distinguishing real experimental military technology from later claims of hidden, highly advanced "secret saucers".<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/195801/AFmuseum/avro-canada-vz-9av-avrocar/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalmuseum.af.mil">[Air Force Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalmuseum.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

## Why designers built a circular vertical-flight vehicle

The Avrocar emerged during the 1950s, when military planners were searching for alternatives to conventional runways. Cold War strategy assumed that fixed airbases might be destroyed early in a conflict, creating demand for aircraft that could lift vertically from dispersed locations and then transition into fast forward flight.

Engineer John Frost and his team at Avro Canada believed that a circular aircraft could meet these requirements. Rather than relying on wings for lift during take-off, the design placed three turbojet engines inside the disc to drive a large central lift fan, or "turborotor". Air was directed downward around the edge of the vehicle, creating lift and allowing the craft to hover close to the ground before transitioning into conventional flight. Research at the time suggested that a circular wing and distributed airflow might provide efficient lift in every direction while avoiding the limitations of helicopters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/195801/AFmuseum/avro-canada-vz-9av-avrocar/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalmuseum.af.mil">[Air Force Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalmuseum.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

This unusual engineering logic explains why the aircraft genuinely resembled the popular image of a flying saucer. The disc was not decorative. It formed part of the propulsion and lift system itself. From a distance, observers would have seen a nearly symmetrical circular vehicle with no obvious fuselage, tail or conventional wings, making it visually unlike any operational aircraft of its era.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/news/article-display/article/110252/declassified-document-shows-real-flying-saucer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air ForceDeclassified document shows &#x27;real&#x27; flying saucer &gt; Air Force &gt; Article DisplayOctober 23, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2012</span></span></span>

## What the Avrocar could and could not do

The project's ambitions were extraordinary. Early concepts envisioned a vehicle capable of vertical take-off, high altitude and speeds well beyond those achieved during testing. Military planners hoped for a machine that combined the flexibility of a helicopter with the speed of a jet aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/news/article-display/article/110252/declassified-document-shows-real-flying-saucer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air ForceDeclassified document shows &#x27;real&#x27; flying saucer &gt; Air Force &gt; Article DisplayOctober 23, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2012</span></span></span>

Reality proved very different.

Testing quickly exposed several fundamental problems:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The air cushion beneath the vehicle became unstable once it rose more than roughly one metre above the ground.
* Pilots encountered severe pitch and roll oscillations known by the engineers as "hubcapping".
* Wind tunnel testing demonstrated poor aerodynamic stability during forward flight.
* Maximum speed reached only about 35 mph (56 km/h), far below the original expectations.
* Engineers were unable to eliminate the instability despite multiple design revisions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/195801/AFmuseum/avro-canada-vz-9av-avrocar/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalmuseum.af.mil">[af.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalmuseum.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on af.mil.</span></span></span>

</div>

NASA evaluations later identified several technical shortcomings, including insufficient thrust relative to weight, aerodynamic instability, inadequate low-speed control and losses within the ducting that reduced the effectiveness of the lift system. The programme ultimately demonstrated how difficult it was to convert a hovering lift-fan concept into a practical aircraft using the technology available at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19940011245/downloads/19940011245.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ntrs.nasa.gov">[NASA Technical Reports Server]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ntrs.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Technical Reports Server NASA</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Contractor Report 177616 The Lift-Fan Powered...</span></span></span>

## Why the Avrocar fuelled flying saucer speculation

The Avrocar illustrates why genuine [classified programmes]({{ 'secret-projects/' | relative_url }}) sometimes became entangled with UFO stories.

Several factors encouraged confusion:

* **Its appearance matched popular expectations.** Unlike conventional aircraft, it was genuinely circular and looked remarkably similar to fictional flying saucers depicted during the 1950s.
* **The programme was classified.** Public knowledge of the aircraft was extremely limited while development was underway.
* **It operated during the height of UFO interest.** The late 1950s saw widespread public fascination with unidentified flying objects, making unusual aircraft easier to misinterpret.
* **Observers lacked context.** A witness seeing a hovering disc-shaped vehicle would have had no obvious reason to assume it was a military experiment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/news/article-display/article/110252/declassified-document-shows-real-flying-saucer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air ForceDeclassified document shows &#x27;real&#x27; flying saucer &gt; Air Force &gt; Article DisplayOctober 23, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2012</span></span></span>

At the same time, the Avrocar's documented performance places clear limits on what it can explain. Flight testing showed a noisy, unstable vehicle confined largely to low-altitude hover. It did not demonstrate the rapid acceleration, extreme manoeuvrability, silent operation or high-speed flight frequently attributed to unexplained UFO reports. ## How its failure complicates claims about secret saucers

The Avrocar occupies an unusual position in discussions of classified aerospace projects because it simultaneously supports and weakens arguments about hidden military technology.

On one hand, it confirms that governments genuinely funded unconventional disc-shaped aircraft. Anyone claiming that "no military ever tried to build a flying saucer" would be demonstrably wrong. Declassified records show that the United States and Canada invested years of research into precisely such a concept.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.af.mil/news/article-display/article/110252/declassified-document-shows-real-flying-saucer/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: af.mil">[U.S. Air Force]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Air ForceDeclassified document shows &#x27;real&#x27; flying saucer &gt; Air Force &gt; Article DisplayOctober 23, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 23, 2012</span></span></span>

    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
