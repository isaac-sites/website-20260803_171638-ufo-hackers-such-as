## Introduction

Gary McKinnon's decade-long fight against extradition to the United States ultimately turned on a single question that had not been decisive in his earlier legal challenges: whether sending him to the US would create such a severe risk of suicide that it would breach his human rights. By October 2012, after extensive psychiatric evidence and independent medical assessments, Home Secretary Theresa May concluded that extradition would expose McKinnon to an unacceptably high risk of taking his own life. She therefore withdrew the extradition order, ending one of the United Kingdom's most closely watched extradition cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

This outcome was not a finding that McKinnon had committed no offences, nor was it based on his claims about searching for UFO evidence. Instead, it reflected a narrow but significant human-rights judgment: in the exceptional circumstances of his [mental health]({{ 'mental-health/' | relative_url }}), the foreseeable consequences of extradition were considered incompatible with the UK's obligations under the European Convention on Human Rights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

## How McKinnon's mental health entered the legal battle

For several years, McKinnon's legal arguments focused primarily on challenging the extradition process itself. As the case progressed, however, increasing attention was paid to his psychiatric condition.

Medical specialists diagnosed McKinnon with Asperger's syndrome (now generally regarded as part of autism spectrum disorder) together with depressive illness. His legal team argued that these conditions made him unusually vulnerable to the stresses of imprisonment, separation from his family and relocation to another country for trial. Psychiatrists warned that these factors substantially increased the likelihood of self-harm or suicide if extradition proceeded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

By the time Theresa May inherited responsibility for the case, the legal question before her had narrowed considerably. The ordinary statutory appeals had already been exhausted. Her remaining responsibility was to determine whether extradition would breach McKinnon's human rights because of his medical condition. To answer that question, she reviewed reports from McKinnon's own clinicians while also commissioning independent medical advice from specialists recommended by the Chief Medical Officer. She also obtained detailed legal advice before reaching a decision.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

## Why earlier appeals did not stop removal

The eventual success of the medical evidence can appear surprising because McKinnon had already lost numerous legal challenges.

Earlier courts generally accepted that extradition treaties required strong respect for international cooperation in criminal prosecutions. They were not persuaded that the anticipated prison conditions or prosecution in the United States reached the exceptionally high threshold needed to prevent extradition under human-rights law.

Crucially, later medical evidence presented a different picture. Rather than arguing that extradition would merely be stressful or harmful, clinicians assessed that McKinnon faced a genuinely high and foreseeable risk of ending his own life. That shifted the focus from general objections to extradition towards a highly individual assessment of his health and personal vulnerability.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

The legal threshold remained demanding. British authorities repeatedly emphasised that mental illness alone does not prevent extradition. The decisive issue was whether, on the evidence available, removal would create an unacceptable risk that the state could not lawfully ignore.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

## Why Theresa May blocked the extradition in 2012

On 16 October 2012, Theresa May announced her decision to Parliament.

She acknowledged that McKinnon was accused of serious criminal offences but distinguished that issue from the separate legal question she had to resolve. After reviewing medical evidence from multiple clinicians, obtaining independent assessments and considering legal advice, she concluded that extradition would create "such a high risk" of McKinnon ending his life that proceeding would be incompatible with his human rights. She therefore withdrew the extradition order.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

Her statement is notable because it carefully framed the decision as an exceptional one rather than a general weakening of extradition policy. She simultaneously reaffirmed that extradition remained an essential tool for tackling cross-border crime and emphasised that McKinnon's alleged offences remained serious. The ruling did not amount to an acquittal or a judgment on the evidence against him.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

Instead, the decision reflected a balancing exercise between the [public interest]({{ 'public-interest/' | relative_url }}) in prosecution and the state's obligation not to expose an individual to treatment incompatible with fundamental human rights.

## What happened after the extradition was stopped

Once the extradition order was withdrawn, responsibility shifted to the Director of Public Prosecutions (DPP), who had to decide whether criminal proceedings should instead be brought in the United Kingdom.

In 2013, the Crown Prosecution Service concluded that a domestic prosecution would not be in the public interest and that there were substantial practical obstacles, including the location of most evidence and witnesses in the United States. As a result, McKinnon was not prosecuted in Britain either.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

The case nevertheless had wider consequences. Theresa May announced her decision alongside the government's response to a broader review of UK extradition arrangements, and the controversy surrounding McKinnon's case became one of several factors influencing subsequent debate over reforms, including stronger consideration of whether some cases should be tried in the UK rather than abroad.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

## Why this decision remains significant

McKinnon's case is frequently cited because it illustrates how human-rights protections can affect extradition without deciding a defendant's guilt or innocence.

The central lesson was that psychiatric evidence must be specific, credible and supported by detailed clinical assessment. It was not McKinnon's autism diagnosis alone that halted extradition, nor his claims about searching for UFO material. Rather, the decisive factor was the Home Secretary's conclusion—after reviewing both independent and treating clinicians' evidence—that extradition would create an exceptionally high and foreseeable risk of suicide.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/news/theresa-may-statement-on-gary-mckinnon-extradition" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Theresa May statement on Gary Mc Kinnon extradition</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Theresa May statement on Gary McKinnon extradition - GOV.UKOctober 16, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 16, 2012</span></span></span>

    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
