## Introduction

Within claims about UFO-related secrecy, hidden antigravity technology is among the most extraordinary assertions because it implies not merely an undisclosed aircraft but a working physical principle capable of transforming transport, energy and defence. If such technology genuinely existed, the evidential standard would have to be exceptionally high—not because the claim is unpopular, but because it would overturn or substantially extend well-tested areas of physics and engineering. The key question is therefore not whether unusual documents, leaked phrases or witness accounts exist, but what combination of evidence would allow independent experts to conclude that an entirely new propulsion technology had been demonstrated.

## Why Isolated Files and Phrases Are Insufficient

Within discussions inspired by figures such as Gary McKinnon, individual screenshots, filenames or references to terms such as "antigravity" or "non-terrestrial officers" are often treated as potential clues. On their own, however, they cannot establish the existence of revolutionary technology.

There are several reasons for this:

* Technical terminology can be misunderstood or used internally in unexpected ways.
* [Classified projects]({{ 'misidentified-projects/' | relative_url }}) frequently employ misleading [code names]({{ 'code-names/' | relative_url }}).
* Individual digital files may be incomplete, altered or taken out of context.
* Without authentication, electronic documents are vulnerable to forgery or manipulation.

Even authentic government documents do not automatically prove that the underlying claims are true. History contains many examples of speculative research proposals, unsuccessful engineering programmes and exploratory concepts that never produced operational technology.

The same reasoning applies to alleged photographs or isolated videos. An image may show an unusual aircraft or unidentified object, but it does not demonstrate the physical mechanism responsible for its flight.

## The Evidential Chain from Hardware to Replication

A convincing demonstration of hidden antigravity would require multiple independent forms of evidence supporting one another.

The strongest evidential chain would include:

1. **Authenticated physical hardware.** Investigators would need direct access to an actual propulsion device rather than descriptions or photographs.


<img src="{{ "/assets/images/ufo_hackers_such_as_e2dece_suppressed_ufo_techn_17b864_hidden_antigravity_p_c40cbd-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Proof Standard illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/ufo_hackers_such_as_e2dece_suppressed_ufo_techn_17b864_hidden_antigravity_p_c40cbd-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/ufo_hackers_such_as_e2dece_suppressed_ufo_techn_17b864_hidden_antigravity_p_c40cbd-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">

2. **Documented provenance.** The origin of the hardware would need to be traceable through a documented [chain of custody]({{ 'chain-of-custody/' | relative_url }}), ensuring that investigators know where it came from, who handled it and whether it could have been altered. Such procedures are standard practice in forensic evidence management because they reduce opportunities for contamination or substitution. [NIST Computer Security Resource Center](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/chain_of_custody)

3. **Complete technical documentation.** Engineering drawings, manufacturing records, materials specifications, operating manuals and development histories would help demonstrate that the device is a genuine engineered system rather than a fabricated artefact.
4. **Controlled performance testing.** Independent laboratories would need to measure thrust, power consumption, heat generation and environmental conditions using calibrated instruments while eliminating known sources of experimental error.
