## Introduction

The names **Hillenkoetter** and **Curtis LeMay** are now deeply embedded in some retellings of Gary McKinnon's UFO story, where they are presented as the names of mysterious "space ships" or "USSS" vessels listed in secret military records. The available evidence, however, points in a different direction. McKinnon's earliest public interviews do **not** include those names, and when directly asked what the vessels were called, he repeatedly said he could not remember. The association of "USSS Hillenkoetter" and "USSS Curtis LeMay" appears to have emerged later within UFO circles rather than from McKinnon's original testimony. Understanding how those names entered the narrative illustrates how memorable details can become attached to a story despite lacking support from its primary witness.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/theguardian/2005/jul/09/weekend7.weekend2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Guardian Game over &#124; Gary Mc Kinnon &#124; The Guardian</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianGame over &#124; Gary McKinnon &#124; The GuardianJuly 9, 2005...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 9, 2005</span></span></span>

## What McKinnon said when asked for vessel names

The earliest detailed public account remains Jon Ronson's 2005 interview with McKinnon. McKinnon described finding references to fleet-to-fleet transfers and unfamiliar ship names, but when Ronson asked what those names were, his reply was unequivocal: he could not remember them, joking that his cannabis use at the time had not helped his memory. That admission has remained one of the most consistent elements of his account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/theguardian/2005/jul/09/weekend7.weekend2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Guardian Game over &#124; Gary Mc Kinnon &#124; The Guardian</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianGame over &#124; Gary McKinnon &#124; The GuardianJuly 9, 2005...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 9, 2005</span></span></span>

Subsequent interviews expanded on what McKinnon believed he had seen—a spreadsheet or workbook containing "Non-Terrestrial Officers" and transfer records—but did not supply forgotten vessel names. In later interviews he estimated that there had been roughly eight to ten unfamiliar vessels, yet continued to say that he could not recall their identities. Even while expressing his belief that the documents pointed towards a covert programme, he acknowledged uncertainties about exactly what he had viewed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://projectcamelot.org/lang/en/gary_mckinnon_interview_transcript_en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: projectcamelot.org">[Project Camelot]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">projectcamelot.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on projectcamelot.org.</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because it separates McKinnon's own recollections from details introduced by later authors and commentators.

## How Hillenkoetter and Curtis LeMay entered later accounts

The names themselves carry obvious symbolic value within UFO history.

* **Roscoe H. Hillenkoetter** was the first Director of the Central Intelligence Agency and later became publicly associated with civilian UFO organisations after leaving government service.
* **Curtis LeMay** was the influential US Air Force general closely associated with Strategic Air Command and Cold War military power.

Both men are already prominent figures in UFO literature for reasons unrelated to McKinnon's hacking claims.

The earliest widely circulated references connecting these names to McKinnon's alleged spacecraft appear to have emerged years after his first interviews. UFO writers and speakers began referring to vessels called **USSS Hillenkoetter** and **USSS Curtis LeMay**, often presenting them as examples of the mysterious fleet McKinnon had supposedly discovered. A frequently cited example is a 2010 discussion that described the names as circulating rumours rather than documented quotations from McKinnon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufohackers.org/hackers/gary-mckinnon/mckinnon-overview" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohackers.org">[UFO Hackers]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohackers.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hackers Mc Kinnon Overview &#124; www.ufohackers.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Hackers Mc Kinnon Overview &#124; www.ufohackers.org</span></span></span>

The names gained further visibility when speakers on the UFO conference circuit repeated them as though they were established facts. For example, a conference transcript records retired Command Sergeant Major Bob Dean describing alleged transfer orders involving "USSS Curtis LeMay" and "USSS Roscoe Hillencotter" as though these were known details from McKinnon's discoveries. The transcript provides no documentary evidence that the names originated with McKinnon himself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://projectcamelot.org/lang/en/bob_dean_awake_and_aware_en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: projectcamelot.org">[Project Camelot]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">projectcamelot.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project CamelotProject Camelot &#124; Bob Dean LA Awake and Aware Conference transcriptSeptember 19, 2009...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 19, 2009</span></span></span>

As these talks, articles and graphics circulated online, the names became increasingly detached from their uncertain origin. Later summaries often presented them without explaining that they were absent from McKinnon's earliest testimony.

## McKinnon's own response to the famous names

The strongest evidence against attributing these names directly to McKinnon comes from his own later statements.

Researchers who have followed his interviews note that when McKinnon was specifically asked during a question-and-answer session following his 2017 MUFON Symposium presentation whether "USSS Hillenkoetter" and "USSS Curtis LeMay" were the names he had seen, he said that they did not ring a bell and reiterated that he simply did not remember the vessel names. Accounts summarising that exchange consistently report that he did not endorse the later attributions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufohackers.org/hackers/gary-mckinnon/mckinnon-overview" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohackers.org">[UFO Hackers]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohackers.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hackers Mc Kinnon Overview &#124; www.ufohackers.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Hackers Mc Kinnon Overview &#124; www.ufohackers.org</span></span></span>

This is significant because it was an opportunity for McKinnon to confirm a detail that had already become popular within UFO culture. Instead, he maintained the same position he had expressed since at least 2005: he remembered finding unfamiliar ship names but not what they actually were.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/theguardian/2005/jul/09/weekend7.weekend2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Guardian Game over &#124; Gary Mc Kinnon &#124; The Guardian</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The GuardianGame over &#124; Gary McKinnon &#124; The GuardianJuly 9, 2005...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 9, 2005</span></span></span>

## Why memorable names become false attributions

The evolution of the Hillenkoetter and LeMay story illustrates a common process in the development of folklore surrounding disputed events.

Several factors encouraged the names to spread:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* They referenced well-known historical figures already associated with military or UFO history.
* They transformed an incomplete memory into a vivid, concrete narrative.
* Once repeated across interviews, conference talks, blogs and social media, later authors often cited previous retellings instead of tracing the claim back to McKinnon's own interviews.
* Readers encountering only later versions could easily assume the names had been part of McKinnon's original account.

</div>

This does not necessarily indicate deliberate fabrication. Details often become standard through repetition, especially when the original evidence is incomplete. In historical research this process is sometimes described as source contamination, where later accounts influence collective memory more strongly than the earliest available testimony.

## What the evidence supports

The available evidence supports a relatively narrow conclusion.

  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
